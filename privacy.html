Privacy Policy

Hermes — personal automation tool. Effective 10 November 2026.

1. Scope

This policy covers the application named “Hermes_1” (the “Application”), which is operated by a single individual for their own personal use. There are no other users, no customers, and no third-party accounts.

2. What data the Application accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, and only for the owner's own Google account, the Application may access:

  • Files and folders in Google Drive that belong to the owner;
  • Spreadsheets owned by the owner;
  • Calendar events of the owner;
  • Email messages in read-only mode.

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to produce summaries, registers, and reports for the owner. The Application does not sell, rent, licence, publish, or transfer data to any third party. It does not use data for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models that are shared with others.

4. Where data is stored

  • Credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored locally on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner's operating-system user.
  • Processed documents remain in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No copy of the data is uploaded to any server operated by the Application's author.

5. Third-party services

The Application communicates only with Google APIs (Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Gmail) using the owner's authorisation. No analytics, telemetry, or advertising SDKs are included.

6. Retention and deletion

The owner may revoke the Application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored credentials. Files created by the Application remain in the owner's Drive and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

7. Security

Access is limited to the minimum scopes required (email is read-only). Credentials are kept in a permission-restricted local file, and no credentials are transmitted to any party other than Google's own token endpoint.

8. Children

The Application is not directed to children and processes no data relating to them.

9. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
