Hermes

A private, single-user automation tool.

Hermes is a personal command-line assistant used by one person (its owner) to organise their own work: reading their own email, their own calendar, and files in their own Google Drive, and producing summaries and reports for that same person.

What this application is

  • It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no user accounts other than the owner's, and it is not distributed to third parties.
  • It runs locally on the owner's own computer and connects to Google APIs using the owner's own OAuth authorisation.
  • All data it processes belongs to the owner. Nothing is sold, published, or shared with anyone else.

Why it requests Google permissions

  • Google Drive — to read documents the owner needs summarised and to save generated reports back into the owner's own folders.
  • Google Sheets — to record incoming correspondence in the owner's own tracking spreadsheet.
  • Google Calendar — to show the owner's own schedule.
  • Gmail (read-only) — to find relevant messages. The application never sends, deletes, or modifies email.

Data handling in one sentence

Everything stays on the owner's machine and in the owner's own Google account.
